## Primary Message

- AiT USA Institute is presented as a non-traditional ESL school.
- Main promise: learn techniques to understand English without translating and to speak without memorizing thousands of words.
- Modalities: in person, hybrid, and online.
- Method: Graphic Concept / GC, described as a proprietary visual method developed from 20+ years working with the community.
- Goal communicated by the public site: students can reach an intermediate level in about 10 months when they persist and follow the method.
