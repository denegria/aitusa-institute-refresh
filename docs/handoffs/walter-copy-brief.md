# Walter Copy Brief - AIT USA Product Refresh

Date: 2026-06-28
Requested by: Giuseppe
Owner: Walter

## Audience

Spanish-speaking adults, parents, and international students considering English classes through AIT USA Institute.

Primary audience segments:

- Adults in New Jersey who need English for work, interviews, school, services, or daily life.
- Parents looking for English help for children.
- Students outside the United States who need online classes.
- Students who have tried apps, videos, memorization, or traditional classes and still feel blocked when speaking.

## Objective

Write Spanish-first conversion copy for a product-led website funnel. The site should feel practical, credible, and human. It should move students toward placement, course exploration, registration/book purchase, or advisor contact.

## Strategy Frame

Homepage sequence:

1. Pain/problem hook.
2. AIT USA solution and three differentiators.
3. Offerings/products.
4. Locations.
5. Testimonials/proof.
6. Final CTA.

Primary promise:

AIT USA helps students stop translating word by word and start practicing English with structure, visual method, teacher correction, and continuity.

## Source Constraints

Use only claims supported by current material:

- AIT USA has existed since 2004.
- Public site describes a non-traditional ESL school.
- Method: Graphic Concept / GC.
- Focus: understand English without translating and speak without memorizing thousands of words.
- Modalities: in person, hybrid, and online.
- Teachers are described as bilingual and North American in captured content.
- Programs include English, English online, kids English, Spanish for foreigners, GED, math tutoring, basic computing, office computing, computer repair.
- Testimonials/videos exist and should be referenced generally as real student proof.
- Registration + book offer is `$95`.
- Future language picker is expected, but this first copy pass should be Spanish-first.
- AI tutor/study assistant language may appear only as "coming soon."

Do not invent:

- Guaranteed fluency.
- Exact job outcomes.
- Exact test-score outcomes.
- New testimonial quotes.
- New location details.
- Live AI tutor availability.
- Payment capabilities.
- North Plainfield address/status.

Future-facing AI wording may be used only as "coming soon" or "future study support" if included.

North Plainfield should not be treated as a normal open location until the real address/status is provided.

## Deliverables

### 1. Hero Copy

Produce:

- 5 headline options.
- 3 subheadline options.
- Primary CTA options for placement test.
- Secondary CTA options for advisor contact.
- Tertiary link copy for detailed courses.

Direction:

- Lead with the student's pain, not the institution.
- Avoid cleverness.
- Make the problem concrete: "entiendo la clase, pero no hablo", "traduzco todo", "no tengo tiempo", "necesito ingles para trabajar".

### 2. Three Differentiators

Write one concise block for each:

- Graphic Concept method.
- Guided practice/live correction.
- Personalized continuity/study support.

For each:

- Short label.
- Title.
- 1-2 sentence body.
- Proof/credibility line.

### 3. Offerings Copy

Write cards for:

- In-person English - flagship.
- Hybrid English.
- Online/digital English.
- Kids English.
- GED.
- Computing/office/technical support.

Each card:

- Title.
- Best-for line.
- 1 short body paragraph.
- CTA.

### 4. Final CTA Copy

Write copy for:

- "$95 registration + book" primary CTA.
- Contact/advisor secondary CTA.
- Placement test CTA.
- Detailed courses page CTA.

Tone:

- Clear, direct, not salesy.
- "Start here" energy.

### 5. Optional Bilingual Microcopy

Only if useful, provide a compact English fallback for:

- Hero headline.
- Placement test CTA.
- Courses CTA.
- Contact CTA.

## Output Format

Use:

1. Audience + intent.
2. Primary draft.
3. Variants.
4. Rationale.
5. Next-step notes.

Avoid markdown tables.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Spanish-first.
- Specific and credible.
- Every major section has a clear action.
- No unsupported claims.
- Copy is ready for Builder/Senior Builder to place into `src/content.js`.
